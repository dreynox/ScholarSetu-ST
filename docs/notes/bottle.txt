- student_id (UUID, Foreign Key -> tribe_students.id)
- amount (NUMERIC/FLOAT, Disbursement amount in INR)
- payment_status (VARCHAR/TEXT, e.g. PENDING, PROCESSING, SUCCESS, FAILED)
- transaction_reference (VARCHAR/TEXT, Bank transaction reference)
- utr_number (VARCHAR/TEXT, Unique Transaction Reference)
- payment_date (TIMESTAMPTZ/DATE, Disbursement timestamp)
- bank_name (VARCHAR/TEXT, Disbursing or Beneficiary Bank)
- failure_reason (VARCHAR/TEXT, Detailed reason if failed)
- created_at (TIMESTAMPTZ)
- updated_at (TIMESTAMPTZ)

Relationships:
- tribe_students.id -> tribe_disbursements.student_id
- tribe_applications.id -> tribe_disbursements.application_id
